# Product foundation

## Promise

4444555 is a curated modern browser arcade for short, high-mastery sessions. Premium means coherent art direction, polished motion and audio, responsive controls, readable effects, reliable performance, and a satisfying complete loop—not simply high polygon counts.

## Release-zero boundaries

The static portal provides discovery, concepts, documentation, and future game loading. Authentication controls are intentionally inactive until a backend is configured. No fake profiles, scores, or leaderboards ship.

The first playable is **Gravity Courier**, subject to Visual Gate 01. Other launch candidates are Echo Vector and Prism Siege.
